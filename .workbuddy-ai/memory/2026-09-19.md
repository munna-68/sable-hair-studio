### Why the seam is genuinely seamless
Verified numerically, not by eye: per-pixel diff of the two PNGs gives
mean abs diff **4.07/255 across the background** (encoding noise) and the
optimal integer alignment search returns **dx=0, dy=0** — the frames are
already registered. Only the hair region differs (18.4/255). Because both files
are the same 1536×1024, `object-fit: cover` crops them identically at every
breakpoint, including the mobile 1:1 square. Confirmed visually in Chromium at
3× zoom on the join, plus desktop (1440) and mobile (390).

### Known follow-up (offered, not applied)
`before.png` 2.08 MB + `after.png` 1.88 MB = **3.96 MB** of PNG replacing a
237 KB JPEG. Measured JPEG re-encode: q86 → 177 KB + 138 KB = **315 KB total,
12.6× smaller, PSNR 42.1 / 44.1 dB** (visually lossless for photos). Lazy
loading mitigates this for now, but these should be converted before launch.
