## The gutter math (the bit worth remembering)
**The rail was 54px wide, and every asymmetric left padding in the file is
`right + ~54px`.** That is the whole reason the layout has a lopsided inset —
it was reserving the rail's lane. E.g. `.page-hero` left
`clamp(5.6rem,12vw,15.5rem)` (172.8px @1440) vs right `clamp(2rem,8vw,11rem)`
(115.2px) → delta 57.6px. So "reclaim the gutter" = **make left equal right**,
which is both simpler and more robust than subtracting 54px in a `calc()`.

Changed to `clamp(2rem,8vw,11rem)` on both sides:
`.service-intro`, `.page-hero`/`.booking-header`/`.about-hero`, `.press-strip`,
`.service-row`, `.booking-layout`, `.visit-layout`, `.notfound-wrap`,
`.filter-inner`, `.site-footer`. `.hero-copy` left → its own right
`clamp(2rem,6vw,7rem)`. `.header-inner` left → `clamp(2rem,8vw,11rem)` so the
brand mark sits exactly above the hero eyebrow (verified: both compute to
115.2px @1440); its right stays 4.5rem. The 1050px media query's
`padding-left: 5.6rem` overrides all became `3rem` to match their rights.
