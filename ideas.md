# Sable Hair Studio — Design Direction

## Three possible approaches

### 1. Chromatic Cut
**Very Brief Intro:** A fresh, mineral-toned studio identity with sculptural image crops, cobalt punctuation, and intentional offset composition. It feels precise and contemporary rather than conventionally “luxury salon.”

**Probability:** 0.073

### 2. The Sunlit Workshop
**Very Brief Intro:** An airy, tactile craft-led direction inspired by product labels, daylight studios, and hand-finished materials. It makes the salon feel candid, local, and deeply competent.

**Probability:** 0.041

### 3. After-Hours Gallery
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet nocturnal art-world direction using saturated blue-black space, reflective materials, and cinematic portraiture. It treats every appointment as a tailored creative session.

**Probability:** 0.086

## Selected approach — Chromatic Cut

### Design Movement
**Contemporary Swiss-influenced art direction with expressive salon-editorial cropping.** The site couples graphic clarity with a small, human sense of motion: strong type, deliberate white space, and images that feel observed instead of stock-staged.

### Core Principles
1. **Clarity is luxurious:** service, timing, and availability are always easy to read before decoration.
2. **Asymmetry with purpose:** sections shift their content edge or image treatment to create rhythm without sacrificing scanability.
3. **Tactile precision:** subtle shadows, hairline-blue strokes, and soft mineral surfaces suggest a considered in-studio experience.
4. **Human expertise first:** the stylist, consultation, and service decision-making are the visual and verbal center of gravity.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a cool **porcelain-blue white** that feels clean and well-lit without becoming generic white. A deep ink blue anchors reading and signals assurance; a saturated **Cobalt Current** accent identifies decisive interactions, availability, and moments of transformation. Pale eucalyptus and a muted coral note appear only as utility signals, never as a broad decorative palette.

### Layout Paradigm
The site is built as an **editorial runway** rather than a centered stack of cards. A slim vertical information rail appears on wide screens; major sections alternate between left-anchored editorial copy and large, off-grid photographic frames. Booking uses a calm, sequential studio desk layout with an always-visible appointment summary.

### Signature Elements
1. **The Cobalt Stroke:** a short, angled blue line marking sections, active states, and selected appointment steps.
2. **Soft-corner photo windows:** only imagery uses generous rounded corners; interface panels stay crisp to preserve contrast.
3. **The studio rail:** date, location, or service metadata sits vertically at the edge of key compositions on desktop.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction gives a concise, practical response: selected services and times visibly accumulate in the booking summary; buttons compress slightly on press; plan comparison reveals the next scheduled visit instead of merely showing a purchase state. Functional confirmation replaces gratuitous animation.

### Animation
On page load, the hero image makes a short, restrained lateral settle while headline lines fade upward in 40ms steps. Section imagery uses 220ms opacity and transform transitions on enter and hover. Cards lift only 2px, and the Cobalt Stroke extends on active or hover states. All nonessential movement is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Space Grotesk** carries high-contrast headlines and navigation with purposeful, slightly geometric forms. **DM Sans** handles body copy, booking interfaces, prices, and policies for dependable readability. Headlines use compact line-height and selective all-caps metadata; body copy stays sentence case with generous line-height. No default system-like headline treatment and no Inter.

### Brand Essence
**Sable Hair Studio is a clear-eyed color and cut studio for people who want their hair plan to be as personal as their final look.**

**Personality:** exacting, warm, modern.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and quietly evocative; CTAs are specific about the next commitment; microcopy reassures with practical detail rather than generic enthusiasm.

> “Color with a point of view — and a plan.”

> “Choose a service, then meet the right chair for it.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark pairs a wide-set SABLE with a tight, small STUDIO; the logo mark is an abstract **S** made from two offset, tapering hair-strand arcs. It is used as a compact cobalt symbol at a clearly visible size in the header and favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Cobalt Current — #2A5BFF**. A cool, energetic blue that makes booking and decision states unmistakably Sable.
